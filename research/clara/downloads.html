Clara FastPass: Free

FastPass is a free tool to potentially pass prop firm evaluations in one day. Read the instruction guide before you consider using it. Clara Systems accepts no responsibility for prop firm rule violations, account losses, or any outcome resulting from the use of this tool. Use at your own risk.

FastPass runs on two evaluation accounts at once. At a time you set, one account enters long and the other short, each with a take profit and a stop loss. If the market moves cleanly in one direction, one account may hit the profit target and pass its evaluation, while the other fails. Getting funded is the objective, and it carries real risk and real cost you need to understand first.
