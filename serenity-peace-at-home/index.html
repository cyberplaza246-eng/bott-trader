Who We Are

About Us

At Serenity Peace at Home, we provide transitional housing and supportive services for women and children experiencing homelessness, hardship, or life transitions. Our goal is to create a safe environment where families can heal, gain stability, and build a brighter future.

Learn More About Our Mission

Our Services

What We Offer

Comprehensive support to help families transition from crisis to independence.

🏠

Safe Transitional Housing

Secure, comfortable living spaces where women and children can feel safe and begin their healing journey.

💼

Employment Support

Job readiness training, resume assistance, and connections to local employers committed to second chances.

🧠

Counseling & Wellness

Individual and group counseling, trauma-informed care, and wellness programs for mind and body.

📚

Life Skills Training

Budgeting, parenting workshops, cooking classes, and practical skills for independent living.

🔑

Housing Assistance

Guidance through the path to permanent housing, including rental assistance and landlord partnerships.
