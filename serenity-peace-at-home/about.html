About Us

Empowering women. Building futures. Creating a place to heal, grow, and thrive — together.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

At Serenity Peace at Home, we provide transitional housing and supportive services for women and children experiencing homelessness, hardship, or life transitions. Our goal is to create a safe environment where families can heal, gain stability, and build a brighter future.

We believe that every woman and child deserves dignity, safety, and the opportunity to thrive. Our trauma-informed approach recognizes that healing is not linear — it is a journey that requires patience, compassion, and consistent support.

Founded on the principle that stable housing is the foundation of all other progress, we walk alongside each family from their first day of crisis through their transition to permanent independence.

Looking Forward

Our Vision

We envision a community where no woman or child faces homelessness alone. A community where every family has access to the resources, support, and opportunities they need to transform struggle into strength — and fear into hope.

How We Help

Our Approach

Serenity Peace at Home uses a holistic, wraparound service model designed to address the many dimensions of a family's needs during transition:

  • Transitional Housing — Safe, furnished units with shared community spaces and 24/7 support staff.
  • Case Management — Dedicated case managers who create individualized plans and coordinate all services.
  • Children's Programs — After-school support, tutoring, and enrichment activities for children of all ages.
  • Workforce Development — Job training, GED support, and employer partnerships for sustainable employment.
  • Aftercare Support — Continued mentorship and resources after families move into permanent housing.

Our average program length is 12–18 months, though we tailor timelines to each family's unique circumstances and progress.
